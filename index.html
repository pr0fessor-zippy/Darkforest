He Won’t Stop

Night. A forest with no trail. Five lanterns burn somewhere between the trees. Find them all and the way out opens. A pistol is hidden out there too, with one bullet. Don’t let him catch you.

WASD to move, Shift to sprint, mouse to look (click to capture it). On touch, left thumb moves and right thumb looks. Click or press Space to fire once you find the pistol. Headphones help.
